3. Target User

User Profile

The target user is a student, recent graduate, or early-career professional interested in pursuing an analytics-related career within the pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing industry. The user may have foundational experience with data analysis, statistics, business analytics, or related disciplines and some exposure to tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, R, or data visualization platforms. However, they may have limited experience within the pharmaceutical industry and may not yet understand how their existing skills align with available analytics roles.

User Needs

Before applying for analytics positions in pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing, the target user needs a clearer understanding of the types of analytics roles available, the technical and professional skills employers commonly request, typical experience and education requirements, salary expectations, geographic and remote work patterns, and potential skill gaps. This information can help the user evaluate their current qualifications and identify which skills or experiences they should prioritize to become a more competitive candidate.

4. Product Question

Primary Question

How can an aspiring analytics professional evaluate career opportunities in pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing and determine which skills, qualifications, and experiences to prioritize to become a competitive candidate?

Supporting Questions

  1. What analytics roles appear most frequently in pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing?
  2. Which technical and professional skills are most commonly requested?
  3. How do salary, experience, location, and remote-work patterns vary?
  4. Where does the target user have skill gaps?
  5. Which skills should the target user prioritize?

5. Project Rationale

Problem Statement

Students and early-career professionals may not have a clear understanding of the skills and job-market needs required for analytics roles within pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing, which can result in difficulty identifying appropriate roles and determining how well their qualifications align with employer expectations.

Why the Question Matters

Understanding these requirements is important because students and early-career professionals may need more than general analytics knowledge when preparing for a career in a specific industry. Knowing which skills, qualifications, and experience employers are seeking can help job seekers make more informed decisions about their career preparation and identify areas they may need to develop before applying. It also enables them to better communicate their own skill sets to align with market needs.

Value of the Product

The finished product will provide job seekers with the insights and information needed to better position themselves in the job market, assess their skills and identify areas for self-improvement, and better communicate their strengths based on what employers are seeking. By bringing together information about available roles, required skills and qualifications, salary, location, and work arrangements, the product can support users in their job search and help them better understand current market needs.
