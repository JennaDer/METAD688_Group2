Market Baseline

Analytics Careers in Computer Systems Design and Related Services

Purpose

This page describes the current market for the team’s Data Analytics career pathway within Computer Systems Design and Related Services.

NoteAnalytical Scope

Industry: Computer Systems Design and Related Services NAICS code: 5415 Career pathway: Data Analytics Analysis period: March 25, 2026–June 29, 2026 Final dataset size: 287 cleaned job postings

Market Overview

The cleaned market panel contains 287 Data Analytics-related postings within Computer Systems Design and Related Services (NAICS 5415). The postings span a broad mix of analytics, data engineering, data science, business analysis, and business intelligence roles. Geographic information is available for most postings, although 82 records are listed as Unspecified. Work-arrangement information is more limited: 145 postings (50.5%) have an Unknown classification, while 65 (22.6%) are Remote, 54 (18.8%) are Hybrid, and 23 (8.0%) are On-site.

Key Findings

  • Demand: The cleaned NAICS 5415 panel contains 287 relevant postings.

  • Location: California has the largest identified state count with 45 postings, followed by Texas with 21. Washington, New York, and Virginia each have 18.

  • Work arrangement: 65 postings (22.6%) are Remote, 54 (18.8%) are Hybrid, and 23 (8.0%) are On-site. Another 145 postings (50.5%) are Unknown.

  • Data coverage: Location and work-arrangement findings should be interpreted with caution because 82 postings have an Unspecified state and more than half of postings have an Unknown work-arrangement classification.

Geographic Patterns

Leading Hiring Locations

Among the 287 relevant postings, California has the largest identified state count with 45 postings (15.7% of the full panel), followed by Texas with 21 (7.3%). Washington, New York, and Virginia each account for 18 postings (6.3% each). Illinois contributes 11 postings (3.8%), Georgia 10 (3.5%), and Massachusetts and New Jersey each contribute 9 (3.1%).

A total of 82 postings (28.6%) are classified as Unspecified for state, so the geographic distribution should not be interpreted as a complete picture of where all opportunities are located.

Interpretation:

The identified postings show activity across several major technology and business markets rather than concentration in a single state. California leads the observed distribution, but the substantial Unspecified category limits the strength of state-level conclusions.

Education Requirements

The source extract does not contain usable minimum or maximum years-of-experience values. Both experience fields are blank across all 165,386 raw postings, so experience requirements cannot be analyzed from this dataset. Following the documented data-preparation approach, education is used as the available qualification measure instead.

The analysis uses MAX_EDULEVELS_NAME because the minimum education field behaves like a modeled floor rather than a stated employer requirement.

Master’s and bachelor’s degree categories together account for most of the maximum education levels recorded in the panel. Remote, Hybrid and Onsite Work

Work-Arrangement Distribution

Of the 287 postings, 65 (22.6%) are explicitly classified as Remote, 54 (18.8%) as Hybrid, and 23 (8.0%) as On-site. The remaining 145 postings (50.5%) are classified as Unknown.

Interpretation:

Remote and hybrid opportunities are clearly present in the market, together accounting for 119 postings (41.5% of the full panel). However, the Unknown category represents more than half of all postings and should not be interpreted as onsite work. Because of this missing classification, the dataset does not support estimating the true overall share of remote, hybrid, or onsite opportunities.

Analytical Limitations

  • The 287-posting panel is a filtered sample of relevant NAICS 5415 postings and should not be treated as the entire national labor market.

  • Salary data are available for 112 postings (39.0%), while years-of-experience fields are blank across the full 165,386-row source extract. Education is therefore used in place of experience requirements.

  • The panel includes multiple related analytics role types, including data analytics, data science, data engineering, business analysis, and business intelligence, so findings represent a broad Data Analytics career pathway rather than a single job title.

  • Geographic and work-arrangement fields are incomplete: 82 postings have an Unspecified state and 145 postings (50.5%) have an Unknown work-arrangement classification.

  • Skills data appear truncated in the source extract, so skill-demand conclusions should be interpreted cautiously until the dataset issue is clarified.
